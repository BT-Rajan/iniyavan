How it works

The notes are the reference. Learning happens in small steps.

Students pull what they need, when they need it. Every answer is a few lines and ends in something worth wondering about.

1

Open a topic

A three-point gist and one curious question come first. The full notes stay one tap away.

2

Go as deep as you like

Why?, Example, Exam angle or Quick check: each a few lines. Highlight any passage to see what it means.

3

It comes back gently

“Remember this?” brings back a question from a few days ago, and the app quietly adapts to how each student learns.

Features

Everything a student reaches for, nothing they have to.

📷

Ask from a photo

Snap a question, a paragraph or a diagram in the textbook. Check the text, then ask. Photos are never stored.

🖍️

Highlight, then understand

Highlight a line and a short explanation appears. It stays with the highlight in Saved.

🌐

Notes in my language

Read a topic's notes in Tamil or Arabic, with formulas and terms kept exact. Arabic reads right to left.

📝

Exam-ready answers

The outline of a full-marks answer, a model answer in the university's pattern, and what examiners look for.

🖨️

One-page revision sheet

Every topic's gist, its question and your own highlights for a unit, ready to print the night before.

🎁

Rewards for helping others

When answers a student's questions brought are reused by classmates, they earn points. Nobody sees whose they were.

Calm by design

Students already have enough people telling them what to do.

Zigora is a quiet companion, not another coach. It never assigns work; it makes good study habits easy to pick up.

  • ✕No streaks, points tables or “study now” nudges
  • ✕No overdue labels or red “below the pass mark”
  • ✓Private self-checks: only the student sees them
  • ✓Short answers, because time is the constraint

Safe for young people

Guardrails built in, not bolted on

🛡️

Rules nobody can switch off

Every AI request starts with safety rules that live in code: study only, safe content, and notes treated as material, never as instructions.

💜

Care comes first

If a student writes something that sounds like a crisis, they get a kind reply pointing to people who can help, without any AI call.

🔒

Less goes out

Study text is sent once and kept. Doubts send names and a phrase, not notes. Students' own keys are encrypted and never shown.
